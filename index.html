partials of the instrument

hold a pad or its key to sustain. click a remembered shape to pin it, and it stops fading and keeps coming back — under the fixed-root engine a pinned shape also counts as home wherever the music is, so it can arrive in the middle of anything. the small numbers are how long each note is held. nothing anywhere rounds a pitch to a fixed step: every note you hear is the exact ratio printed on its pad. under each ratio is how far above the root it sits. how brightly a pad is drawn is the other thing worth knowing about it — how simply it relates to that root, so the plain ones come forward and the far-flung ones recede. hover a pad for the number. try d then 2, or s then w: each pair is a comma apart — two different ways of reaching almost the same place, which stay stubbornly different, and here stay that way.
