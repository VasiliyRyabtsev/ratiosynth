ratiosynth

every note is an exact fraction of another, and nothing is rounded to fit a keyboard.
hold a pad or its key to sustain, shift for an octave up and alt for one down · under each ratio is how far above the root it sits, and the simpler the ratio, the brighter the pad

the field — every note a wave, and where they cross

each note is one wave. the simpler the ratio, the broader its bands — the same thing the pad's brightness says; the higher the note, the faster it slides; and notes reached the same way lean the same way. press g, s, 2 — built on 3, leaning alike, at 2½ then 6 then 12 bands. then press d: almost the same pitch as 2, and a completely different picture.
go inside the field is this same picture, with the eye inside it.
